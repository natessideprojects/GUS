02 / GUS DIY

Make a drive
that knows your world.

GUS DIY is the generic edition: a small core library plus the readable reference files you choose. Build a local knowledge drive for a hobby, workshop, project or personal field library.

Bring your own useful.

Collect your manuals, notes, saved web pages, PDFs and GPS files. GUS preserves the reference files, extracts readable content, builds a local index and retrieves source passages when you search or ask.

Adding files builds an index; it does not retrain the language model or turn imperfect source material into verified advice.

FROM EMPTY FOLDER TO USEFUL LIBRARY

Build it around what matters to you.

  1. Give the full app a home

    Extract the complete DIY demo to a new folder on your Windows computer, USB drive or external SSD. Keep the launcher, System and OWNER_FILES together.

  2. Collect a small starter library

    Choose a few documents you have permission to use. Clear, searchable text is easier to verify than a large stack of scans. Keep your originals and backups elsewhere.

  3. Add and scan

    Copy supported files into the top-level OWNER_FILES folder. Open GUS DIY, choose My Files, then Scan My Files. Refresh alone does not import documents.

  4. Ask one concrete question

    Start with a question whose answer you can check in a known document. Inspect the numbered citation; for a PDF, use View Cited Page to check the original image.

  5. Test, refine, back up

    Check edits, rescans and restart behavior on a test library. Add files gradually. Safely eject a portable drive after the app has closed and all writes finish.

Ideas for your own drive.

01

A workshop reference shelf

Your equipment manuals, inspection notes and parts information. Check exact specifications against the original page.

02

A hobby or project library

Organize your own reference notes, saved pages and readable PDFs around the topics you return to.

03

A personal route collection

Bring your own GPX, KML or KMZ material. Imported geometry is a reference; it does not confirm current conditions or permission.
