A SIMPLE ROUTINE

Pack it. Open it. Check the source.

01

Prepare your copy

Download a complete demo, extract it to a Windows folder or drive, or install the North Georgia APK on a supported Android phone.

02

Make it useful

Browse the included material or import your own documents and GPS files. Scan them, then search or ask a focused question.

03

Keep a human in the loop

Open the numbered citations and original pages. Answers can be incomplete or wrong; a map line does not establish legal access.
