THE SMALL PRINT, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Keep the references.
Keep the notices.

GUS combines its own interface and branding with third-party software, models, maps and reference material. Those components keep their own terms and attribution.

Third-party content travels with the apps.

Keep the bundled LICENSES and runtime notice directories when copying or sharing a demo. They include notices for the local model, llama.cpp, dictionary material, map engine/data, routing data, voice and other dependencies where supplied. Third-party licenses are not replaced by this website or by the demo label.

Relevant components include Gemma (its terms and prohibited-use policy), Organic Maps and OpenStreetMap data, Wiktionary contributors through Kaikki, and packaged runtime dependencies. Sources and reference records inside the app provide further attribution. Check the license files for the specific component and build.

The project owner confirmed completion of the redistribution review on November 7, 2026. Older personal-preview wording is retained in the original package documentation as build history. These downloads are offered as testing demos, not as a finished or professionally validated product.

Your files and this website.

The website does not ask you to upload your library, run the model in your browser or create an account. This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to measure visits and page views in aggregate, including pages, referrers, approximate regions, browsers and device types. It does not use analytics cookies or session replay. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Vercel and linked services may process ordinary connection information under their own policies. See Vercel’s analytics privacy information.

The apps copy or index your files locally as described in their own Help and terms. Android uses private app storage; Windows uses its portable folders. Optional external links, email, GitHub downloads and Ko-fi open those services. Review a document’s sensitivity and permissions before importing or sharing it.
